How the estimate is produced

  1. Segment the leaf from the background (vegetation response + Otsu).
  2. Keep the largest connected component as the leaf mask.
  3. Classify lesion pixels by hue/saturation/value cues (brown, necrotic, chlorotic).
  4. Report the affected fraction of the leaf area.
Severity represents the estimated visible affected leaf area and should not be interpreted as ground-truth disease severity.
