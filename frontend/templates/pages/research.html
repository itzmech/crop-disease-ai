Dataset

PlantVillage

A public benchmark of crop-leaf photographs organized by crop and disease, used to train and evaluate all four architectures.

The PlantVillage images are captured under controlled conditions; models trained only on them can degrade on field photographs. The methodology therefore emphasizes explainability and honest uncertainty rather than leaderboard accuracy alone.
Pipeline

End-to-end methodology

01

Data & preprocessing

Images are resized to each architecture's input resolution, normalized, and split into train/validation/test partitions with stratification by class.

02

Augmentation

Random flips, rotations and brightness/contrast shifts improve robustness to lighting and orientation differences in real photos.

03

Model training

Four architectures are trained under comparable budgets: a Custom CNN and three transfer-learning backbones (ResNet50, MobileNetV2, EfficientNetB0) with class-balanced loss.

04

Evaluation

Test-set accuracy, precision, recall and F1 are computed once per model and registered as JSON; the dashboard only ever displays those measured files.

05

Explainability

Grad-CAM attributes each prediction to spatial regions by weighting the last convolutional feature maps with class gradients.

06

Severity estimation

An image-processing branch (leaf segmentation → lesion colour cues → affected-area ratio) estimates visible severity independently of the classifier.

Architectures

Four models under study

Baseline

Custom CNN

A compact convolutional network trained from scratch — 3–5 conv blocks with batch normalization and dropout. Establishes the reference point that transfer learning must beat.

Transfer learning

ResNet50

Residual connections ease training of very deep networks on small datasets; the frozen-backbone-then-finetune recipe is applied with a new classification head.

Edge-oriented

MobileNetV2

Depthwise-separable convolutions and inverted residuals trade a little accuracy for a large reduction in parameters and latency — the deployment candidate.

Compound scaling

EfficientNetB0

Neural-architecture-search-derived scaling of depth, width and resolution; typically the strongest accuracy-per-parameter trade-off in the study.

Theory

How Grad-CAM works

For a predicted class c, gradients of the class score are propagated to the final convolutional feature maps A^k. Global-average-pooled gradients α_k^c weight each map, and a ReLU over the weighted sum yields a coarse localization:

L^c = ReLU( Σ_k α_k^c · A^k ),  α_k^c = (1/Z) Σ_i Σ_j ∂y^c / ∂A^k_ij

Because the attribution is computed from the actual model on the actual input, every heatmap on this site is evidence — not decoration.

Ethics & limitations

What this system is not

  • Severity is a visible-area estimate, not ground-truth disease load.
  • Low-confidence outputs are flagged, not hidden — they are part of honest science.
  • No result is fabricated when models or files are missing; the UI reports unavailability.
  • Outputs are research demonstrations, not agricultural or economic advice.
